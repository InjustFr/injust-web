## Data processed, purposes and legal bases

### Contact messages and quote requests

- Data: name, email address, subject and content of the message, and any information you choose to include.
- Purpose: answering your request and, where relevant, preparing a quote.
- Legal basis: pre-contractual steps taken at your request or, failing that, the legitimate interest in answering the messages received.
- Retention: three years from your last contact.

### Orders and invoices

- Data: name, email address, billing address and country, country of issue of the payment card, where applicable company name and VAT number, order details, amounts and download history.
- Purpose: performing the order (lesson, service or licence), sending the confirmation, download links and invoices, handling any withdrawal or complaint, and keeping the accounts.
- Legal basis: performance of the contract and compliance with legal accounting and tax obligations.
- Retention: ten years for invoices and accounting records (article L123-22 of the French Commercial Code); five years after the end of the contract for the rest of the order file.

### Consents and contractual evidence

- Data: the boxes ticked at checkout (request for early performance, waiver of the right of withdrawal, acceptance of the licence), with their date, time and the exact version of the text shown, as well as withdrawal requests and their acknowledgements.
- Purpose: proving the conclusion and the terms of the contract.
- Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation and the legitimate interest in establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.
- Retention: five years after the end of the contract.

### Sign-in to the site administration

- Data: identifier, email address and groups provided by the authentication service, session cookie and remember-me cookie.
- Purpose: restricting the site administration to authorised people.
- Legal basis: legitimate interest in securing the site.
- Retention: the length of the session for the session cookie; thirty days for the remember-me cookie; the administrator account (identifier, email address, groups) is kept for as long as the person keeps access to the administration.

### Technical logs

The server records technical logs (IP address, date, page requested) to keep the site secure and working, on the basis of legitimate interest. They are kept for at most one year.
